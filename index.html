Who We Are

From a Four-Partner Firm to One of MP’s Fastest Growing Contractors

VVC Realinfra Private Limited — formerly V.V. Construction — began in June 2006 as a partnership between close friends, and was corporatised on 1st April 2013 under Part IX of the Companies Act, 1956. What started with a turnover of ₹2.03 crore has grown into a business with a peak annual revenue of ₹305 crore in FY 2023–24.

The experienced quartet of Mr. Vinod Kumar Lahoti, Mr. Virendra Kumar Agrawal and Mr. Narendra Kumar Lahoti, joined by a next generation of engineers, architects, lawyers and managers, has taken the company into almost every major branch of the construction industry — roads and highways, development works, civil and structural works, buildings and industrial projects — for more than twenty reputed government clients.
