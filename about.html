About VVC Realinfra

Eighteen years of civil engineering, from a four-partner construction firm in Raghogarh to a CRISIL BBB+ rated infrastructure company operating across Madhya Pradesh.

The Company

VVC Realinfra Private Limited

VVC Realinfra Private Limited (VVCRIPL), formerly V.V. Construction (VVC), is a private limited company incorporated under Part IX of the Companies Act, 1956 (No. 1 of 1956) with effect from 1st April 2013, after taking over all of the assets and liabilities of the firm M/s. V.V. Construction.

The original partnership firm came into existence on 28th June 2006 and began business with the collaboration of close friends and partners at the outset. New partners were subsequently added on 13th March 2013, after which VVC was corporatised and converted to VVCRIPL on 1st April 2013 under section (U/s) 574 of the Companies Act, without dissolution. The financial record of the exiting firm is therefore proprietary data of VVCRIPL, which in turn holds all the obligations and rights of the earlier firm.

Since inception, the vastly experienced quartet of Mr. Vinod Kumar Lahoti, Mr. Virendra Kumar Agrawal and Mr. Narendra Kumar Lahoti, together with the addition of next-generation members, has pushed the company through many prestigious projects in almost all major fields of the construction industry — roads and highways, development works, civil and structural works, buildings works, as well as industrial projects for a range of reputed clients.

Our Journey

Eighteen Years of Steady Growth

Beginning in the year 2006 with a maiden turnover of ₹2.03 crore, the company has logged a maximum annual revenue of ₹305 crore in the financial year 2023–24 — a long way from its humble beginnings to becoming one of the fastest growing construction companies in Madhya Pradesh.

  • 2006

    V.V. Construction is founded on 28th June as a partnership of close friends. First-year turnover: ₹2.03 crore.

  • 2013

    New partners join in March; the firm is corporatised on 1st April as VVC Realinfra Private Limited under Part IX of the Companies Act, 1956.

  • 2016–17

    Lawyers’ Chambers at Arera Hills, Bhopal is named 2nd Best Project of the Bhopal Development Authority.

  • Landmark

    Central India’s largest multi-level car parking at New Market, Bhopal is inaugurated by Hon’ble Prime Minister Sh. Narendra Modi.

  • 2023–24

    Peak annual revenue of ₹305 crore; more than ₹1,300 crore of projects executed over five financial years.

  • Today

    CRISIL BBB+ rated, with an order book of approximately ₹1,500 crore and 25+ projects under execution.

People & Culture

Manpower Is Never a Hindrance

VVCRIPL has sufficient manpower resources including civil, mechanical, administrative, accounts, finance and other support personnel for the smooth running of operations and unfettered execution of all types of construction projects. The company employs a dedicated and talented workforce of over 400 competent staff from more than 16 states, with varied backgrounds and skill sets, and is currently engaged in 25+ projects at various stages of completion.

The company never lets manpower or finance become a hindrance in the execution of any project through efficient management of resources at hand and prudent financial planning.

VVCRIPL takes great care of its employees by providing the best facilities like food and accommodation, transport convenience and various other amenities. Almost 50% of the current employee strength has clocked over five years of service with the company — a testament to the fact stated above. The attrition rate is as low as 3%, vastly superior to that of the industry and among VVCRIPL’s peers as well.
