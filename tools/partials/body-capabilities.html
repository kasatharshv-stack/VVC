What VVCRIPL Executes

The company works in almost every major field of the construction industry, for government agencies and departments, consultants and private majors across multiple locations.

01

Roads & Highways

Strengthening and upgradation of national and state highways, rural road networks under MPRRDA, and ideal (ADARSH) road packages for MPPWD. Largest executed: 84.2 km of NH-86 between Sagar and Chhatarpur at ₹88.6 crore.

02

Buildings & Mass Housing

Residential towers with all allied services and facilities, commercial shops and affordable housing under PMAY and JNNURM. Largest executed: 1,432 flats across 20 ten-floor towers at Bhuri Tekri, Indore.

03

Green Buildings

Distinguished experience in composite works for green administrative buildings — the GRIHA-certified GAIL building at Kailaras (₹11.7 Cr), the first in Madhya Pradesh, and the GAIL building at Chainsa (₹11.6 Cr), among the first few green buildings of GAIL in Haryana.

04

Auditoriums & Cultural Buildings

VVCRIPL is the only local infrastructure firm qualified for constructing 1500+ capacity auditoriums in Madhya Pradesh, with three auditoriums delivered including the largest indoor auditorium in the state.

05

Multi-Level & Smart Parking

The distinct credit of having built Central India’s largest multi-level car parking — among the first semi-automated smart parking buildings in India, integrating a three-storey commercial complex with lift provision.

06

Civil, Structural & Industrial Works

Development works, water resource structures and industrial packages, executed directly and as a sub-contractor to national construction majors.
